# Real Steel (2011)

**Real Steel** is a science-fiction sports drama film starring **Hugh Jackman**.

### Plot Summary
In a near-future world where human boxing has been replaced by robot boxing, a down-and-out former boxer named *Charlie Kenton* finds an old sparring robot named **Atom**. Together with his estranged son *Max*, they train Atom to compete in the high-stakes world of robot fighting.

> “Sometimes the underdog is the one who surprises everyone.”

### Main Cast
- **Hugh Jackman** as Charlie Kenton
- **Dakota Goyo** as Max Kenton
- **Evangeline Lilly** as Bailey Tallet
- **Anthony Mackie** as Finn

### Why It’s Great
1. Amazing robot fight scenes
2. Strong father-son story
3. Underdog theme that hits hard
4. Cool mix of sports and sci-fi
